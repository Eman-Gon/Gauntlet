Hackathon Tracks
These tracks are directional, not restrictive. You can build within a track, combine tracks, or go your own way.
💼 Agents
Build focused AI workers that can take on real jobs people or companies would pay to outsource.
The goal is simple: one agent, one job, done well. The agent should be discoverable and hireable through AgentBox the moment it ships.
⚙️ Workflow and Operations Agents
Build agents that automate repetitive business tasks, backend workflows, internal processes, and execution-heavy work.
These should replace the manual, recurring labor that drains time inside real companies.
⚡ Marketplace-Ready MVPs
Build practical agent products that can be deployed, listed, and hired directly through AgentBox.
These should be designed for the marketplace from the first commit — packaged, priced, and ready for buyers.
🃏 Wildcard
Build any agent-native concept that solves an intense real-world problem.
If it does not fit neatly into the tracks above but solves something real, build it.
